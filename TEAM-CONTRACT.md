## How we decide
Small things: whoever is doing the work decides. Big things (scope, stack, pivots, anything in `docs/INTERFACES.md`): majority vote on Teams; on a 2–2 tie, the owner of the role the decision touches breaks it. Big decisions get a record in `docs/decisions/`.

## When we work
Weekly sync: on Teams after the weekly lab. Core hours we can all be reached: 18:00–22:00 on weekdays.

## Contribution
Work is visible in commits, pull requests and the journal. If someone carries or someone coasts, we say it in week terms, not in Week 15. Every change to `main` goes through a pull request approved by another member.

## When it breaks
Step 1: name it inside the team within 48 hours. Step 2: if unresolved in one week, we bring it to the instructor together. No-show rule: after two missed syncs without notice, the team raises it with that member directly; after a third, we bring it to the instructor.
