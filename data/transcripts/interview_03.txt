# segment: mobile coffee cart operators
# date: 2026-10-12

Interviewer: Tell me about the last event you worked.
Customer: A university fair two weeks ago. We ran out of cups after three hours and had to close early.
Interviewer: How much do you think that cost you?
Customer: About 600 GEL in sales, that was a good day until the cups ran out.
Interviewer: How do you keep track of supplies now?
Customer: A free Google Sheet on my phone, but I forget to update it when it is busy.
Interviewer: Do you pay for any software or services for stock?
Customer: No, nothing. I do not pay for any tools, money is tight with a cart.
Interviewer: Would you use an app that reminded you to reorder?
Customer: Probably, yeah.
Interviewer: When you run short before an event, what do you do?
Customer: I drive to the cash-and-carry the night before and buy extra cups and milk, it takes about two hours.
Interviewer: Honestly, do you think this is a good idea?
Customer: It sounds like a good idea, I am sure lots of people would want it.
Interviewer: Is there anything you would spend money on to fix this?
Customer: For events I would pay maybe 20 GEL a month, but only if it works offline, the signal at fairs is terrible.
