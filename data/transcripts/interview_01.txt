# segment: independent cafe owners
# date: 2026-10-10

Interviewer: Tell me about the last time you ran out of an ingredient.
Customer: Last Saturday we ran out of oat milk by 11, lost maybe twenty orders.
Interviewer: Wouldn't an app that predicts stock be great for you?
Customer: Sure, sounds nice.
Interviewer: How do you track stock today?
Customer: A paper sheet on the fridge, and I text my supplier every Thursday.
Interviewer: Do you pay for anything to help with that?
Customer: We pay 45 GEL a month for a POS add-on, but it doesn't do inventory.
Interviewer: How often does running out happen?
Customer: Milk and croissants, maybe twice a month. Coffee beans almost never, I keep two extra bags.
Interviewer: What did you do the last time it happened?
Customer: I sent my barista to the supermarket and we paid retail, almost double the supplier price.
Interviewer: Have you tried any other tools for ordering?
Customer: I tried a free inventory app last year but stopped after a week, entering everything by hand took too long.
Interviewer: You'd love it if all of that was automatic, wouldn't you?
Customer: Of course, who wouldn't.
Interviewer: Anything else you want to tell me?
Customer: Just that Saturdays are the hardest, the morning rush is when we run out.
